Best fit

For roofing companies that can handle more opportunity.

The best fit is an established roofing business with a defined service area, meaningful job value, and a sales team that can follow up consistently when new opportunities come in.

Established operation You already have the crews, sales process, reputation, and capacity to take on additional work.
Strong service economics Your average roofing job is valuable enough that acquiring another customer can support paid acquisition.
Ready to follow up You have someone responsible for responding to new inquiries and turning legitimate opportunities into jobs.

Process

From targeting to the sales opportunity. We build the system around the whole journey.

1

Research the market

We study your service area, highest-value services, customer profile, competitors, offers, and existing acquisition channels.

2

Build the campaign

We create the offer, ad angles, direct-response creative, targeting, and landing page or lead form around the homeowner we want to attract.

3

Generate opportunities

Meta ads put the campaign in front of homeowners in your market. Interested prospects take the intended next step and their information is captured for your team.

4

Test and optimize

We monitor the campaign, test creative, offers, targeting and conversion points, and improve the system based on what the data tells us.

Our purpose

Build a more consistent source of roofing opportunities without relying on one source of demand.

A

Reach homeowners earlier

Instead of waiting for every homeowner to search for a roofer, paid advertising lets you proactively put the right offer in front of people who may have a relevant roofing need.

B

Optimize for opportunity quality

We are not trying to manufacture the cheapest possible form submission. The campaign is built around the service, homeowner problem, offer, and market that make business sense.

C

Build something repeatable

The purpose is to create an acquisition channel that can be measured, tested, and improved over time rather than depending entirely on referrals or inconsistent demand.

What we don't promise

No guaranteed lead numbers. No pretending we control your sales process.

01

No fixed lead guarantee

Market conditions, offer strength, competition, budget, seasonality, and follow-up all affect results. We will not promise an arbitrary number of leads before testing.

02

We don't manually qualify every lead

The system is designed to attract the right type of homeowner from the beginning, but your team is responsible for contacting, assessing, and closing the opportunities.

03

We show you what is happening

We track the acquisition side of the process and use the results to determine what should be tested, changed, or scaled next.
